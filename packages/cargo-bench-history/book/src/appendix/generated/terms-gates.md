| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **agreement share** | The fraction of before-and-after pairs that agree the level moved in the same direction. |
| **confidence interval** | A range the benchmark engine reports alongside a measurement to say how precisely it pinned it down. |
| **typical residual** | How far a representative point sits from the history model fitted to the series: a level for a change point, a line for a drift. |
