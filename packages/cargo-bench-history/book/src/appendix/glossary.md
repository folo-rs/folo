## A note on what these are not

The definitions above are working descriptions, precise enough to follow what this tool
does. They are not textbook definitions, and several deliberately gloss over conditions
that do not arise here — a rank comparison, for instance, is described in terms of what it
counts rather than what it assumes about the data.

Where the distinction matters to how the tool behaves, the chapter says so at that point.
